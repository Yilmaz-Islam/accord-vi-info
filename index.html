About the event

Five years in. Bigger every time.

Accord VI is Beaconhouse's flagship annual music event, returning for its 6th consecutive year. It opens on 18 October with a live concert and closes on 19 October with a Qawwali Night. Alongside the main stage, daytime activations keep the crowd engaged from morning to night — including a Squid Game experience and an esports competition with prizes up to PKR 15,000.

It's built for students 12 and up, with roughly 20% accompanying teachers, parents and family. Accord V featured Bayaan live and sold over 1,000 tickets — and on Instagram alone, past editions have reached an estimated 25,000–50,000 people.
